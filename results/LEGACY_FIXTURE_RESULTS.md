# LEGACY — synthetic fixture results

Everything in this folder **except `official_rdd2022/`** was produced on the synthetic fixture
(`data/fixture/RDD2022/`), not on the official RDD2022 dataset:

- `audit_summary.json`, `box_statistics.csv`, `class_distribution.csv`, `dataset_statistics.csv`,
  `duplicate_report.csv`, `unsupported_labels.csv`, `split_summary.json`, `smoke_test/` (Week 1–2)
- `week3/` … `week7/`, `final/`

These files are kept unchanged for traceability. Their metrics are near zero and are not evidence
about road-damage detection. Do not cite them as RDD2022 results and do not copy them into
`official_rdd2022/`.

Official-data outputs live only in `results/official_rdd2022/` (gitignored, regenerated by
`scripts/run_official_dataset_pipeline.sh`). Fixture debugging runs made with `--dataset fixture`
go to `results/fixture_runs/`.
